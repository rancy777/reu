# Evidence Fusion Engine

## Modalities

1. Source evidence
2. Sample evidence
3. Experimental evidence
4. Tool/provider evidence
5. Historical case evidence

## Rules

- Independent evidence is stronger than repeated copies.
- Source quality does not override contradictory sample evidence.
- Tool output is an observation; it is not automatically a conclusion.
- Runtime evidence can establish behavior without establishing product identity.
- A signature can generate a protector hypothesis without proving the protector.
- Historical case evidence transfers only when scope matches.
- Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence unless the observation procedure had sufficient coverage.
- Conflicting evidence must remain visible until resolved.

## Fusion procedure

For each claim:
1. list supporting evidence;
2. list counter-evidence;
3. classify each item by source/evidence type;
4. assess independence;
5. assess applicability scope;
6. assess temporal validity;
7. choose a conclusion state:
   - Confirmed
   - Probable
   - Possible
   - Unknown
   - Needs verification
   - Rejected

Do not emit a numeric probability unless a calibrated scoring system is explicitly defined and validated.
